Announcement: Why is the old owner of GhostNet quitting?
May 2026

NothingButTyler will soon be stepping away from the GhostNet project.

This decision comes down to a few key reasons — limited time to continue development, difficulty consistently working on the bot, and some creative differences in the direction of the project.

Rather than letting development slow down further, Tyler has chosen to step away and allow the project to continue under new leadership.

Ownership of both the Discord server and the GhostNet bot will be transferred to br3h, who will take over development moving forward.

This change is not due to any conflict — Tyler and br3h remain on good terms. Tyler may still be around, but will no longer be actively working on the bot.

From br3h:

"I’ll be taking over GhostNet moving forward. I’ll do my best to keep things running and continue improving the bot. Appreciate your patience during this transition."

The project will continue, and updates may follow under new leadership.

We will let you know if anything changes!
